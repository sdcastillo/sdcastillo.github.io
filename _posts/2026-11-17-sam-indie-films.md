## How it was made

### Story writing
Character-roleplay prompts with a Dolphin chatbot. The story grew out of back-and-forth roleplay that locked in tone, beats, and Sam’s point of view before any images were generated.

### Storyboard layer
Visual planning in Google Notebook LM plus hand sketches. This stage mapped the sequence, shot ideas, and mood so the later video generation had a clear path instead of pure improvisation.

### Video generation
Grok Imagine turned the storyboard and reference photos into HD clips. Real photos of Sam were used as the visual anchor so the main character stayed consistent across scenes.

### Grounding with RAG
Retrieval-augmented generation pulled from real photos and notes so the animation stayed tied to actual appearance and context instead of drifting. Sam’s character remains the same throughout. Other characters shift (the waiter in a shark costume, different women on the date, possible wig or color-changing hair). Whoever takes the pictures controls the Instagram narrative; Sam carries the main-character energy.
