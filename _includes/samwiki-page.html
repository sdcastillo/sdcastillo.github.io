We are a team

There’s always someone smarter out there. I know that. My strength is being a people person.

When people gather, combine ideas, and form a team — and the team’s goals rise above any one person’s goals — that is success. This is mutually beneficial.

We are not a zero-sum game. We are a cooperative one.

Pick a level and test yourself. If a demo is confusing, a note skips a step, or you see a better way, fork the repo and open a pull request. That is you joining the team. The next person gets a clearer path, and so do you.

Sam

The challenge ladder

Difficulty is a level, the same way a game asks you to pick one before you play. Choose a rank and test yourself. The line on each card says who the project is for.

Every card has Contribute / Open a PR. Fork the project, make the improvement, and send it back. You are joining the team.

The public index

SamWiki is the home index for Sam Castillo’s open-source work in mathematics, machine learning, and actuarial science. Start here when you want a demo, the source, or a write-up, instead of hunting through GitHub.

Much of it was built while Sam was a mathematics student at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, on his own time, without computer science department resources. The math is the part meant to stay solid. A lot of the interfaces were spare student pages, and those are the ones being brought up to a shared GitHub Pages look.

It is for students, actuaries, and anyone who wants to read the work in the open. A Live link opens a demo on github.io. Source opens the repository. Articles and notebooks stay next to the code they explain.

The catalog is ranked by those four levels. Search by name, language, topic, or who a project is for. Only original public repositories are listed. Forks of other projects stay off this index. Training notes from the previous homepage are on the Running page. Also on this site: Home, About, and Code.

Linktree is the hub for links that live off this site.
